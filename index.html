Goodbye from the Ruhr Uni Bochum

After ten editions, this is the end

emBO++ took place for the first time in 2015 – with four people. From then on it happened every year until 2025, and it grew into an international community of programmers, developers and everyone interested in embedded systems and hardware-related software development.

We filled the days with talks ranging from physics and classical electrical engineering, system design of boards for embedded systems and development toolchains to programming libraries, special features of programming languages and their standardization. And we filled the nights with parties.

After ten editions, emBO++ is over. There will be no further edition. This page stays online as a farewell and as the entrance to the archive of all recorded talks.
